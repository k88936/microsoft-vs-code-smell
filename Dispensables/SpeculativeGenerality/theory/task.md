You get speculative generality when people say, “Oh, I think we’ll need the ability to do this kind of thing someday”
and thus add all sorts of hooks and special cases to handle things that aren’t required.
The result is often harder to understand and maintain.
If all this machinery were being used, it would be worth it.
But if it isn’t, it isn’t. The machinery just gets in the way, so get rid of it.
