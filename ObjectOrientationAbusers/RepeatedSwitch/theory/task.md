Repeated switch is where the same conditional switching logic (either in a switch/case statement or in a cascade of
if/else statements) pops up in different places.
It has the problem that, whenever you add a clause, you have to find all the switches and update them.
Against the dark forces of such repetition, polymorphism provides an elegant weapon for a more civilized codebase.
