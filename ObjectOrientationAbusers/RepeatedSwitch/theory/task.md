Repeated switch,
where the same conditional switching logic (either ina switch/case statement or in a cascade of if/else statements)
pops up in different places,
has the problem that, whenever you add a clause, you have to find all the switches and update them. 
Against the dark forces of such repetition, polymorphism provides an elegant weapon for a more civilized codebase.
